# Project Architecture Rules
Read nested AGENTS.md.

- ThemeProvider fixes light mode without profile/OS hydration. AppLayout's first tenant child is one pointer-inert fixed atmosphere behind sidebar/content; tenant glass tokens include mobile portals and preserve platform styles/overlay stacking.

- Scanner links use `buildMatchLinkPatch` atomically; display copies never alter evidence, AI, validation or saves.
- Scanner prices write the supplier entry first, then sync canonical costs through its conversion. Finance-team figures come only from `_shared/financeTeamReview.ts`; AI wording rejects unknown numbers or finding keys.
- Isolated matcher owns its model, pre-date context and hard checks; admin backtest writes only match_backtest_results, never scanner or financial data. Later mutable master fields use earlier invoice evidence or are omitted (no snapshots).
- Finance-team alone writes immutable finished snapshots.
- Section navigation mounts once in AppLayout; sidebar, mobile and local tabs share route families/effective-user access. Purchasing uses usePurchasingNavigationAccess with server-backed preview roles; People selects the most-specific path. Preserve route guards.
- Purchasing mode is read through usePurchasingMode from tenant-scoped app_config; only ProcurementDashboardTab uses purchasingOverviewRows to bypass the venue picker for central buying, with explicit tenant-scoped reads for both invoices and revenue, so group figures never mix clients or change other pages' scope.
- Purchasing Overview/dialogs share purchasingOverview.ts math; creation time is last upload (no uploaded_at).
- Purchasing budgets use the SECURITY INVOKER save_purchasing_budgets RPC for atomic budget/history writes under RLS; purchasingOverview.ts owns pure budget math and full-month per-venue buildProjectionRows forecast aggregation, and usePurchasingBudgets loads each picked month independently. Page suggestions pass an unsaved initial map into the dialog's editor. Budget history replays per venue/category with Hong Kong day boundaries while preserving untouched venue contributions, so saves cannot split history and group charts do not conflate venues.
- Expenses shares nav/period/scope; confirmed posting is atomic; claims require review and vendor merges preserve locked records.
- Finance shares month checks for Overview/Reports/close; tenant advisory locks guard posted headers/lines, owner reopening is audited, and typed conversions retain sources with unique links to prevent double counting.
- People keeps dated terms/owner/payroll locks; rota/typed hours pay, never clock-ins. Bani shares People drafts/cards; one executor locks owned cards, delegates existing RPCs and audits results. Undo checks state/access/payroll and keeps IDs; posted pay stays fixed.
- Voice routing uses the existing reasoning turn and full conversation, and acknowledgements originate only from confirmed tool work; bounded non-confidential session clips share one cancellable media element. Final speech extracts complete prose without a second model request, keeping text independent of audio.
- Voice capture has one mobile microphone owner (MediaRecorder); wall-clock capture deadlines and abortable stage deadlines must remain independent of analyser levels and recorder start events, with an explicit orb send and recoverable notices instead of discarding quiet recordings.
- Composer dictation uses MediaRecorder + bani-stt (never Web Speech) and only fills the editable draft; the orb has a constant ambient grain/swirl but its level-driven scale and glow come solely from real signals (mic analyser, or PCM envelope at the `<audio>` currentTime), and playback must never be routed through WebAudio.
- Floating Bani is tenant-only with one conversation held in FloatingBaniProvider (shared by global and scanner-hosted instances, reset only on user/tenant/preview change) and relocates into the active scanner host; attachments are read-only analysis in chat-assistant `floatingFiles.ts`; unsaved corrections use server-validated whitelists, supplier-scoped candidates and host-rechecked stable draft/line IDs with mutation revisions, never model-side writes or Save/GRN bypasses.
- Floating Bani classifies attachments (sales report / supplier invoice / expense) and has two navigation actions: `open_sales_scanner` (one file, separate sales slot in `scannerHandoff.ts`, consumed by Sales Data's Scan Receipt) and `open_invoice_scanner`: the server validates the venue and emits an action event, the client hands original in-memory File objects through `src/utils/scannerHandoff.ts` (consumed by request id, re-armed only on real scan failure), and the scanner runs its normal parse pipeline; unsaved reviews are never replaced without explicit user consent, and Save/GRN stay on the existing explicit workflow.
- Sales corrections use salesReview.ts + salesReceiptCorrections.ts: whitelisted, tenant-validated, user-applied to the same draft, never financial writes.
- Bani contextual memory lives in chat-assistant `contextMemory.ts`: past record notes plus `bani_memory_notes` (server-written, venue/supplier scoped) are injected as untrusted background, never as rules; post-scan verdicts come from deterministic scanner checks, not extra model calls.
- Scan Invoice calls match-invoice-lines once per draft/supplier; sure uses buildMatchLinkPatch with stable-line/unchanged-input guards. Manual choices write tenant-scoped match_feedback. Other pages keep their matcher; save guards stay unchanged.
- Scanned sales receipts auto-record only through parse-receipt `intake.ts` using pure rules in `_shared/receiptAutoPost.ts`: figures must balance, venue/date valid, no existing same-venue-day sales or closure, and an independent second model read must agree field-by-field; insert/journal run as the user; otherwise a non-posting intake with reasons.
- The Launchpad (`/`) is the single tenant front door; its drop zone stages files into the shared Floating Bani composer (`useFloatingBaniAttach`) so classification/routing stays in one place. `/assistant` and `/finance-team` remain routable but are not top-level sidebar links.
- Venue filtering is one portal-wide scope (`useVenueScope` in `src/hooks/useVenueScope.tsx`, pure rules in `src/utils/venueScope.ts`) mounted once in AppLayout with the top-bar picker and page reminder; pages must not add their own venue view filters (data-entry venue fields are separate), so every page agrees on which venues are shown.
- Venue seats come only from `venue_seat_history` (via `useSeatHistory` + `src/utils/seatHistory.ts`); every seat-based number uses the count that applied on each day, and `venues.seats` is a trigger-maintained mirror of the latest row, so seat history is never lost.
- Revenue/Purchasing/Stock share SectionPillRow/portaled tabs; Register/GRNs reuse Invoices; lists use usePeriodFilter. purchaseRegister owns item math; stockPresentation owns On hand. StockCountScreen/useStockCount use user-RLS save_stock_count_area for locked area totals/unknowns; stockCountReview owns gaps; read-stock-count reuses MATCHER_MODEL and validated candidates. Sent counts are immutable with whole-count approval; browser speech is stock-only. Downloads filtered; Documents redirects.
- Lists use kit; salesRecordChecks owns Sales checks, menuCosts owns menu math with RLS refresh/bought drafts. supplierAccounts/supplierDeposits own lists/received Assets; invoker confirmations and idempotent returns never sync invoices/POs.
- sales_record_notes stores explanations; Your team accepts Closed early / Weather / Private event / POS problem for drops.
- DailyRecords uses a fixed-column semantic table with optional user-keyed column preferences; missing runs derive from full scoped sales history and tenant-local dates through salesRecordChecks, so period filtering never invents trading start dates.
- Closed days live in `venue_closures` (single days) and `venue_closure_rules` (every-weekday / ranges), resolved only through `src/utils/venueClosures.ts` (client) and the finance-team loader; `venue_closed_days` is deprecated and only kept in sync, so every check agrees on which days were closed.
- Bani action cards: chat-assistant `propose_action` writes a row to `bani_pending_actions` (service-role only, no client grants) and streams an `action_card` event; FloatingBani renders Confirm/Cancel and calls back with `action_id`+`decision`, where the server re-validates ownership, expiry (30 min), single-use status, and re-checks sales conflicts before executing. Card titles for closed days are computed server-side from the validated venue/dates — never trust the model's title. Closed days are only markable for yesterday or earlier (Asia/Hong_Kong).
- Bani must never infer missing sales days from aggregates: `get_missing_sales_days` (chat-assistant) is the authoritative check (no sales AND no closure, through yesterday); `get_venue_closures` exposes closed days so they are never flagged as missing.
- Floating Bani briefing: on first open each day with an empty conversation, FloatingBani sends one hidden briefing turn (per identity+date); the Launchpad (YourTeam) auto-runs the finance-team review once per scope per day when stale, and the pg_cron job `finance-team-scheduled-reviews` (hourly at :05) generates the daily review after 7 AM tenant time via `finance_team_schedules`.
- Invoice review_status: Draft, To approve, Approved, Disputed, Voided (legacy "Under Review" stays readable); scanner saves map via mapScannerReviewStatus (src/utils/invoiceStatus.ts). Drafts are excluded from Purchasing Overview and payables. Disputed invoices hold back invoices.held_amount (per-line max(0, invoiced − accepted)); usePayables outstanding = max(0, total − paid − held). Delivery problems go through DeliveryCheckDialog (problem-only, from invoice or GRN detail): every issue takes an affected quantity, received = current − affected, unaffected lines keep received qty, and saving disputes the GRN + invoice and adds affected × unit price to held_amount (src/utils/deliveryCheck.ts). GRN received_date = invoice date (Hong Kong). tenant_members.purchasing_role is display-only, admin-edited in User Access.
