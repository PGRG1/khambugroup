# Settings
- Settings destinations resolve through settingsNavigation.ts with legacy query compatibility; the page gate retains admin/preview and invoice-only Master data access, so navigation never grants access.
- Focused editors reuse existing tenant-scoped hooks, mutations and atomic RPCs; query destinations change presentation only, and long work renders in the detail pane rather than a containing modal.- Settings is one overview plus one page per destination, same on phone and computer (no side menu or dropdown). Groups, in order: Business · Team & access · Buying & stock · Money · Lists · Bani (settingsGroups). Each overview row shows its current value and anything missing in amber from useSettingsSummary (counts and small reads only). Destination pages show ← Settings, title and description, then their editor in one card; Venues has its own summary card and table, and each venue uses tabs Seats · Service periods · Closures · Stock areas · Location · Status. A venue's account mapping lives only in Finance › Accounting › How things post.
