# Finance rules
- Reports use financeReportViews/FinanceAging for read-only balances, due terms and capped payment proposals; ManagementReports retains typed adjustments.
- Schedule settings and deferred items are tenant-scoped, RPC-written; finance_entry_drafts is read-only to clients. Explicit expense_month_approve selections own schedule posting, with current source and closed-period rechecks; page loads never prepare or post.
- Month close uses server snapshots and append-only explanations; triggers require completeness and block unexplained/matching/correction exceptions. Guarded actions own writes.
- PeriodControl/periodWindow owns Finance date windows and session selections; useBooksWindow resolves Accounting configuration and tax year, and Finance checks only request bounded months so malformed journal dates never create menu years or extra checks.
- Invoice asset proposals route source debit at selected approval; create register/depreciation only then. Posted invoices use compensating drafts, and posting rechecks source fingerprints and counted delivery. Existing sales deferred edits use guarded source RPCs; posted days retain originals with reversal/rebook drafts, FIFO balances rechecked at approval. Previously deferred/grouped sources fail closed until reconciled.
- Bank statements load through utils/bankStatementImport.ts only (Cash › Upload = BankStatementUpload, and the StatementUploadFlow check screen): saved by itself only when every pocket maps to exactly one known account and its lines add up from opening to closing balance; a period already saved for that account is skipped (never imported twice); a failed save removes what it wrote. Loading never touches the books; matching happens afterwards. Card payout reports upload in Card payouts, bills in Expenses › Bills; filenames alone are never trusted as document evidence.
- Schedule RPCs recheck sources at selected approval. Deferred sales bypass normal rebuilds and receipt auto-post. Unpaid bank proposals delegate payment creation and matching only at selected monthly approval with source fingerprints; account merge authorization is independently server-checked, never broadens schedule approval. Tax notices are append-only non-ledger due evidence. Asset bills capitalize during guarded source approval; advance bills fund pending day-based releases only on approval. Central shares are report-only; sales merchant aging uses matched gross tender evidence, leaves ambiguous mappings explicit, and remains separate from ledger aging.
- Foreign currency: `src/utils/money.ts` is the only ISO money formatter; rates come only from `fx_rate(tenant,date,currency)`. Foreign documents retain original_total/rate and base amounts. Bank evidence stays native with explicit base_money fields; settlement insert triggers retain original_amounts and convert once. Existing journal lines are never rewritten. FX payment/revaluation drafts use selected schedule approval with source/rate fingerprint checks and open-period locks; approved revaluation includes its first-of-next-month reversing entry atomically. Missing bank carrying balances and unreconciled deposits fail closed, never invent gain/loss. Dedicated FX accounts remain below operating costs.

- Document currency: total/subtotal/tax/line amounts stay base; document-currency values live alongside (original_total/original_balance, line original_unit_price/original_total, allocation original_amount, credit original_currency_amount/original_remaining, opening original_amount + fx_rate). Null currency = base, so base-only consumers never change.
- Foreign settlements go through SQL fx_settle (document's own rate, last payment clears remaining base, difference to FX-R realised); card-charged foreign documents use fx_card_charge (unposted: rate from bank line; posted: FX-FEE).
- Card payouts: multi-line bank matches link each settlement batch to the bank line, and a shared bank line books each batch at its own net_settlement; the same payout (merchant, settlement/transaction dates, gross, net) is never saved twice (client skip + zz_settlement_batch_no_duplicate trigger).
- Finance follows the Rillet layout: pills Overview · Cash · Receivables · Payables · Close · Accounting (route /finance/books) · Reports; /finance is the Overview. Cash tabs are Accounts · Card payouts (?view=payouts) · Cash boxes (?view=petty). Accounts shows cash you have (banks · tills · cash boxes), then one balance, one plain status (amber needs you, green fine) and one next step per place; bank says (last statement closing) vs books say (posted ledger on that statement day, useCashLedger + utils/cashPosition.ts) sit under Columns · bank vs books; ?account=<id> is the one home for that account's lines to match and its statement months to close (finance_close_bank_period). Old ?view=transactions|statements|match|close links redirect. Reports › Bank reconciliation is the read-only cross-account view. Close has Checklist and Approvals (the former overview queues). Accounting is the only home for chart of accounts, account/category mappings, schedules, tax and currency setup; Settings › Money setup keeps processors, opening balances, books start and Bani posting, and its moved destinations redirect into Accounting.
- Receivables follows Cash: tabs Owed to you · Ageing · Deposits & vouchers. Owed to you shows the total (card payouts · delivery apps · other), then one owed amount, one plain status and one next step per payer; ?payer=<merchant id|contracts> is that payer's page (sale days oldest first, money in clears the oldest days, status Paid / payout report in / No payout report / Expected). Card payout uploads, batches and fee checks live only in Cash › Card payouts. Ageing (ReceivablesAgeing) uses the same oldest-first rule as Reports' AgedReceivables with friendly names. Deposits and vouchers (finance_deferred_items) live in Receivables › Deposits & vouchers via FinanceSchedulesView focus="deferred" (not yet earned by kind, open items, Used · Refund); Accounting › Schedules no longer lists them.
- Bank matching is Bani's job, not a button: loading a statement starts bani-accounting, and a 5-minute schedule (mode scheduled) keeps him going; scheduled runs re-queue asked bank lines every 6 hours so late invoices, bills and payout reports still match. A supplier payment is matched by who was paid (supplier named in the line, with something owed), recorded as one payment and applied oldest first by bani_accounting_commit (0178), leftover stays supplier credit, same rule as supplierPaymentAllocation.ts. Lines with nothing to match yet say 'Waiting for …' (grey); others ask. Cash › account shows lines in statement order with Bani's column (matched · question · waiting · checking).
