# People architecture
- People retains dated terms/locks/RLS. peoplePresentation owns role/pay facts; teamWorking owns reconciled venue allocations/house settings. peopleOverview is read-only; saved pay replaces accrual, no coverage stays unknown, Cook / Runner maps to Kitchen here only. Payroll has no allocations; Finance shares them without writes. peopleRecord deduplicates leave; peopleAttendance separates facts; shared peopleRestDays owns evidence-only debts for views/drafts. Work settlements/confirmations use guarded RPCs; payroll_work drafts are separate until atomic owner approval delegates to payroll_approve. Views never prepare or change saved pay; peopleWork owns display/draft math. Orb uses confirmed RPCs.
